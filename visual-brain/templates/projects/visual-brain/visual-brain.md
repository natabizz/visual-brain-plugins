# Visual Brain

The brand's visual identity: who we shoot for, what the market looks like, and the direction that sets the brand apart.

## How to start

Run `/visual-brain:brand`. You don't need to prepare anything. It reads your brand files, studies your audience and the market by itself, and then shows you what it found and what it could not get.

## What only you can add (optional)

Attach them in the chat when the command asks, or paste links. Nothing is required.
- Brands you look up to, in or outside your category.
- A moodboard, brand book, or images you love or reject.
- Screenshots of pages that block automated viewing (the command names them).

## Where things are

- `wiki/brand/visual-guide.md`: the visual identity.
- `projects/visual-brain/landscape/`: the audience, the market, the frames, the tests.
- `projects/visual-brain/outputs/visual-direction.html`: the page (source with image paths: `visual-direction.src.html`; other languages: `visual-direction.<lang>.html`).
