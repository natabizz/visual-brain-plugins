# Product Visual

Each product's visual: who it is for and what they need to see, its positioning and UVP, a visual direction, and a storyboard of its images with a brief per frame, for wherever the product lives (a marketplace, the brand's site, search, social or ads).

## How to start

`/product-visual:new <product>`. You don't need to prepare anything: it finds what it can and asks only for what it can't. Photos, a label, a size chart, a moodboard: attach them in the chat when it asks.
