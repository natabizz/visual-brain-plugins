# raw/visuals

Image files for the Visual Brain and Product Visual. This folder stays on your computer: it is in `.gitignore`, because competitor and reference images are third-party material and your own photos are large. The `.md` files cite each image by ID and path.

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `own/` | Your own photos, by product |
| `screens/<brand>/<date>/` | Images of other brands and of your own pages, as seen on that date |
| `direction/` | Your moodboards, brand assets, references you like or reject |
| `boards/` | Boards the plugins build for tests and pages |
| `products/<product>/` | Product Visual: product photos, drafts, finished images |

**How files get here:** attach them in the chat when a command asks; the command files them here. You can also drop files into these folders yourself.
