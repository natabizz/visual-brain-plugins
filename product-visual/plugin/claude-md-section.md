## Product Visual

**Each product's visual lives in `projects/product-visual/<product>/`.** Read its `product.md` (facts with sources), `audience.md` (ranked needs), `positioning.md` and `direction.md` before briefing, generating, choosing or judging any image of that product.

- Built from `wiki/brand/icp.md` and `wiki/brand/positioning-messaging.md` (the Marketing Brain) and `wiki/brand/visual-guide.md` (the Visual Brain). When the guide has a chosen direction, every product image follows its look, its cast and its caption rule.
- Order for a new product: `/product-visual:new`, then `/product-visual:gallery` per channel, `/product-visual:qa` on the finished images, `/product-visual:ai-read`, `/product-visual:review`. For a live listing: `/product-visual:audit`.
- Product facts come only from `product.md`, each with its source. Text on a live listing is not a source.
- Fit, size, material, construction and tests are never shown with a generated image. The product is never redrawn, recolored or regenerated.
- Image files live in `raw/visuals/` and never go to git; nor do the built pages, which carry the images inside them (their `.src.html` sources do).
- Nothing is made public, shared, uploaded to a store or channel, or paid for (image generation included) without the owner's yes. Showing the owner a page as a private artifact only they can see is not sharing.
