01

The brief

Fabulous Fido makes open-ear knit hats in different sizes and materials. This example explores how to explain the product and the size choice.

On Etsy and Chewy the brand appears next to novelty prints and one-chart-fits-all basics. The task: find what buyers of these dogs actually struggle with, show where the brand can stand apart, and set a look that says it before anyone reads a word.

Founder-led project. Research, strategy and art direction: the brand team.

03 / The insight

Choosing a size can be difficult.

Body proportions can make a size label alone unhelpful. This draft explores measurements as a clearer way to choose.

Synthesis of historical feedback, not a customer quotation. Individual records and their sources stay in the private working map.

04

The shelf, mapped

Seven brands, 35 product frames. The shelf is loud prints on white seamless. The quiet end of the map is almost empty.

No brand we looked at shows one quiet color in a real moment. That corner is open. Whether buyers want it is the next test, not a given.

Positions are an expert reading of 35 public frames and 55 own frames, 2026-11-12. Prices on the shelf ranged from $12.50 to $70.

05Owned key message / proposed

Fit for dogs built differently.

Sized by the measurements that decide each piece, not by breed or weight. For a hat: head circumference in front of the ears, plus the distance between them.

Their problem

Generic sizes

Cut for an average dog. The chest fits, the back hangs, the neck won't close.

A body measurement is more useful than a size name alone.

Our answer

Measure two things

Head around the front of the ears. Distance between the ears. Pick one size.

Built Differently by Nature.

The proof to build

Ears out, adjustable cord

Ear openings and a separate adjustable chin cord. Fit and movement still need checking in use.

A product fact is not a promise that the hat stays on.

Public feedback is summarized without individual quotations or record identifiers. "Built Differently by Nature" is the brand's line. The key message remains a proposal.

06

Color with character

One named house dog per colorway. Eye level. Ears through the knit. The hat on a matte ground of its own hue.

Five of six colors already work this way. The direction makes it the rule, and fixes the one that breaks it.

Eye to eye

Camera at the dog's eye line. Frontal or a quarter turn. Both ears through and visible. Crown in the upper third, square-safe crop.

Ground in its hue

Matte paper or plaster in the hat's own color, lifted a step. One soft side light with one window-shadow stripe. No props.

One dog, by name

One of four house dogs per colorway, in turn. Ties braided and hanging straight. The dog is the model, not a prop.

08

Made by hand

The Cozy Ears capsule is knit by artisan knitters in Bolivia and designed in the United States.

Real hands, real yarn, real measuring tape. These frames carry the brand's quality story without a single adjective.

"Designed in USA. Handmade in Bolivia." Brand insert card and site copy (raw/brand, 2026-10-13). Applies to Cozy Ears only; Snug Ears is a mill knit.
